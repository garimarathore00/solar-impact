🚀 Space Weather Telemetry System

The Solar Impact Analysis platform compiles massive datasets from NASA solar telemetry, modeling physical hazards associated with high-velocity Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs) and radiant solar flare surges.

These magnetic and electrostatic storms directly endanger human communications, causing global navigation (GPS) drift, orbital satellite deterioration, high-altitude aviation radiation, and massive electrical grid surges.

By parsing spatial coordinates, vector velocities, and geomagnetic indicators, this dashboard maps global vulnerability indices to help developers, scientists, and engineers build resilient smart networks.
